## Project Overview

This is a personal memoir/creative writing project in Traditional Chinese, titled **「曾經有一個人，她是我的綠洲」** (Once There Was Someone, She Was My Oasis). It is Ray's autobiographical account of a first-love relationship spanning 2008–2025.

## File Structure and Relationships

- **`story-base.txt`** — Raw, conversational source notes in the author's own voice. This is the ground-truth narrative; all polished content derives from it.
- **`story.txt`** — The complete polished story, all chapters concatenated in sequence.
- **`Chapter X｜*.md`** — Individual polished chapter files (Chapters 1–17 + `end｜...`), each corresponding to a section of `story.txt`.
- **`綠洲章節目錄.md`** — Table of contents listing all chapters and appendix items.
- **`信.txt`** — A cover letter written to the podcast「馬克信箱」accompanying a story submission.

When editing a chapter, keep `story.txt` in sync (it is the concatenated form of all chapter files).

## Writing Style and Tone

- Language: Traditional Chinese (繁體中文).
- Voice: First-person, reflective, warm, and honest — written as if speaking to a trusted friend, not performing for an audience.
- Prose rhythm: Sentences vary between short punchy beats and longer flowing lines. Preserve this rhythm when editing.
- `story-base.txt` is the raw source. When polishing, preserve the emotional truth and specific details (place names, dates, objects like the blue flip-flops, the hand-knitted scarf) — these are intentional anchors, not filler.
- Do not over-literarize or add metaphors that aren't already implied by the source material.

## Chapter Continuity Notes

- The story spans 2008–2025 and follows a non-linear emotional arc: early romance → separation → reunion cycles → cohabitation → breakup → solo travel → self-recovery.
- Key recurring motifs: 淡水 (July 31st), 楓之谷 (MapleStory), 小貓 (the cat), and the「綠洲」(oasis) metaphor.
- Privacy: some details (names, specific identifying information) have been intentionally blurred with GPT assistance — do not restore or guess real names.
